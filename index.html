Harun Ur Rashid

Research Assistant, AI and Networking Lab, Dept. of ICE, HUFS, South Korea

I am a Reasearch Assistant at AI and Networking Lab in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering (ICE) at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (HUFS). My research concerns use of AI and machine learning in networked communication systems, 5G, 6G wireless network, embodied AI and AI agent for network resource optimization.

My current work spans design and orchestration of embodied AI reference architecutre as standardization perspective. Also, I am working on self-supervision of reinforcement learning for coordinated multipoint in 5G wireless communications

I received my M.Sc in Engineering degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Cukurova University, Adana, Turkiye, in 2021, where I was awarded prestigious Turkiye Burslari scholarship for graduate studies. I am a PhD candidate in dept. of ICE at HUFS, South Korea. I hold a BSc Engineering degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from University of Dhaka, Bangladesh.
