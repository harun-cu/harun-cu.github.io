Harun Ur Rashid

Research Assistant of AI and Netowrking Lab · HUFS Global Campus

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Inforamtion and Communications Engineering at AI and Netowrking Lab of HUFS, South Korea. My research concerns networked communication systems and edge intelligence: how resource-constrained devices can sense, communicate and decide reliably when bandwidth, energy and latency are scarce.

My current work spans task-oriented and semantic communication over low-power wide-area networks such as LoRa, 5G/6G Internet of Things and non-terrestrial networks, vehicular communications (V2X), flying ad hoc networks (FANETs), and applied machine learning at the network edge. Earlier work addressed the Internet of Underwater Things, named data networking, and human activity recognition with ambient binary sensor networks.

I am studying Ph.D. in Inforamtion and Communications Engineering at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, South Korea. Also I have received MSc in Engineering degree from Cukurova University, Turkiye. I hold a B.S. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from University of Dhaka, Bangladesh.
