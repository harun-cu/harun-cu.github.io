Research

We are studying how intelligent wireless networks can make context-aware decisions about scarce communication resources, prioritizing what matters most to users and services, and how to make these decisions reliable across dynamic, heterogeneous, and increasingly autonomous network environments.

Research Themes

AI-Native 5G/6G Networks

Reinforcement learning and intelligent control for adaptive resource management, QoS optimisation, and autonomous decision-making in next-generation wireless networks.

Intelligent RAN & Network Slicing

Urgency-aware resource allocation, multi-agent learning, and QoS-aware slicing for reliable and efficient support of heterogeneous 5G services.

Intelligent Mobility & Handover

Learning-based coordinated multi-point handover management for reducing unnecessary reconfigurations while improving reliability, fairness, and network efficiency.

Offline & Distributed Reinforcement Learning

Reliable policy learning from operational data and distributed agents, addressing exploration risks, reward imbalance, large action spaces, and non-stationary network dynamics.

AI for Future Networks & Standardisation

AI-native network architectures, intelligent network function orchestration, federated learning, and standardisation for terrestrial, mobile, satellite, and emerging autonomous networks.
